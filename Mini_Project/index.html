📦 Product Inventory Dashboard

Welcome to our simple inventory management system!

🛒 Manage Products

Add a new product by entering its name and price in the pop-up boxes.

💰 Inventory Analytics

Calculate the total value of products and find affordable items.

🏷️ Product Categories

Products are categorized as Budget, Standard, or Premium based on price.

📊 How to View Your Results

1. Enter a product name and price in the pop-up boxes.

2. Wait for the inventory processing to finish.

3. Press F12 on your keyboard.

4. Click the Console tab to see the output.
